# Landing (index.html #shop)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: couples planning a wedding, browsing on a phone. Action: choose a design (catalog), secondary: write Valeria in Telegram. Proof: live builder demo, demos of templates, envelope mechanism. No reviews or business numbers until real ones exist.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The invitation is opened, not scrolled to. The first viewport is a wax-sealed envelope that opens and lets the live invitation rise out. Refuses the category default of a phone fan with floating glass chips, marquee and numbered eyebrow sections.

OWN-WORLD: Fine stationery. Warm stone paper ground (#f2ede5) with laid-paper grain, near-black ink (#1b1714), one metal: antique gold of the shell seal (#a3824f) used only for hairlines, seal and price marks. Display: Noto Serif Display light with true italics (Cyrillic, Didone contrast); text: Onest. Hairline rules instead of card borders; pill buttons in ink; small-caps tracked labels only inside controls.

STORY: Visitor sees the envelope open and understands the product in one beat; sees designs with real prices from 1 290 ₴; learns what guests get, how it works, tries the builder, sees the cabinet, picks a plan or writes Valeria.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered headline (2 lines, ~88px desktop), one-line lead, primary "Обрати дизайн" + secondary "Відкрити демо", price note. Below, centered, the envelope (~560px wide) with the seal; flap opens and a phone with Our Story rises. Three facts on a hairline row under it.

FORM: pinned by user reference (thedigitalyes.com: quiet luxury, envelope hero, short structure). seed: none (direction pinned by user reference)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
